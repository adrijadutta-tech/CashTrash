The problem

Most waste ends up in the wrong place

Not because people don't care — because the system doesn't tell them what to do in the moment.

01

No one tells you where it goes

Households and communities routinely misjudge which bin a wrapper, bottle, or scrap belongs in.

02

One wrong item spoils a batch

A single wrongly-sorted item can contaminate an entire batch of otherwise recyclable material.

03

Facilities are hard to find

Residents rarely know the nearest center for e-waste or specialty items, so it ends up in general trash.

Key features

Everything the prototype needs, nothing it doesn't

Scoped for a working MVP — built to grow without a rebuild.

AI waste recognition

Classifies plastic, paper, glass, metal, organic, e-waste, or flags it unknown rather than guessing.

Smart bin guidance

Maps every identified category to the correct bin, with plain-language disposal notes.

Points & rewards

Automatic points for correct sorting, with visible progress toward the next reward tier.

Recycling center finder

Surfaces nearby centers and collection facilities for items that need special handling.

User accounts

Points, scan history, and progress are all tied to a single, secure profile.

Reliable end-to-end flow

Scan history, dashboard, and results stay in sync so nothing gets lost between sessions.
