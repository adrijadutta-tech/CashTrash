CashTrash

An AI-Powered Community Waste Management & Recycling Platform

Snap a photo, Know the bin, Earn rewards, Recycle right.

Problem?

Many people want to recycle correctly but don't know how, they're often unsure which bin an item belongs in, end up mixing recyclable and non-recyclable waste, and have no easy way to find a nearby recycling center for things like e-waste or plastic. Existing apps only offer generic rules or static directories instead of actually looking at the item itself, and there's nothing motivating people to make the effort.
CashTrash solves this by letting users snap a photo of their waste, instantly identifying it, telling them exactly where it should go, directing them to the right recycling center when needed, and rewarding them with points for disposing of it correctly.

AI identifies it
